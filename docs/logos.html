Each is shown large, at 32px and 16px (where most logos die), in a wordmark lockup, and reversed on dark. Toggle the theme in the nav to check both. Tell me a number and I will apply it across the site, the favicon and the report header.

How to choose

Ignore which looks nicest at 104px — every one of them does. Judge them at 16px in the row above, because that is the size a browser tab, a LinkedIn avatar and a report footer will actually render. A mark that turns to mush there will quietly undermine you in every context that matters.

Second test: does it still read when someone has never heard of post-quantum cryptography? 1, 3 and 5 say "security" instantly. 2, 6 and 8 say "quantum" first and security second. 4 and 7 are the most neutral and the most scalable if the company later sells more than this one service.
