The mechanics

Two algorithms.
Very different problems.

Most coverage of this is vague. The real picture is specific, and the difference decides what you have to do.

Shor's algorithm

Solves factorisation and discrete logarithms in polynomial time — precisely what RSA, DSA, Diffie-Hellman, ECDSA and Ed25519 all rest on. Their security isn't reduced against a quantum computer. It's eliminated.

Grover's algorithm

A quadratic speed-up on search, halving symmetric strength. AES-128 drops to about 64 bits and has to go; AES-256 drops to 128 and holds. This half is a configuration change, not a programme.

The consequence

Because traffic can be recorded now and read later, your exposure isn't set by when the machine arrives. It's set by how long your data must stay secret — and for patient records or pension files, that window opened years ago.

The thing most vendors get wrong

A bigger key does not help. RSA-15360 falls to the same algorithm as RSA-2048 — it simply takes marginally longer. Migration means replacing the mathematics, not resizing it. Anyone advising you to "increase key length" for quantum readiness has misunderstood the threat.

What an external scan cannot see

Internal systems, VPNs, SSH, code signing, database and backup encryption, and hardware security modules are invisible from outside — and that's usually where the difficult cases live. The free scan covers your public estate; the paid assessment goes inside, which is what NCSC's 2028 discovery phase actually requires.

NCSC roadmap

Three dates already fixed

2028

Discover and plan

A complete cryptographic inventory of every system that depends on cryptography, and a migration plan. You can't replace what you can't enumerate — which is why this comes first.

2031

Migrate the priorities

The highest-priority work done, starting with anything protecting long-lived confidential data. This is where harvest-now-decrypt-later exposure actually closes.

2035

Finish

Everything migrated. Under NIST's parallel timeline, today's 112-bit algorithms are disallowed entirely from this point.
