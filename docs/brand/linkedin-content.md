## 7. First five posts, after launch

Post roughly twice a week. Each one teaches something; none of them ask for anything.

1. **"A bigger key does not help."** The single most common misunderstanding.
   RSA-15360 falls to the same algorithm as RSA-2048. Explain why in 150 words.

2. **What harvest-now-decrypt-later actually means.** Use the ten-year retention
   example. This is the post that makes people forward it to their boss.

3. **The three NCSC dates, and what each one actually requires.** Practical, not
   alarmist. Position 2028 as the one that needs work starting now.

4. **What an external scan can and cannot see.** Publish the limits honestly.
   Counter-intuitively this builds more trust than a capability list.

5. **Aggregate findings from a cohort scan.** Never name anyone. "We scanned N UK
   councils; X% support post-quantum key exchange." This is the post that gets
   picked up.
